Create one transparent-ready concept sheet on a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for later slicing and transparency processing.

Asset family: shop_scrollbar_parts
Asset type: vertical scrollbar track and thumb for secondary shop menus
Target paths:
- assets/source/ui/events/shop/secondary/shop_scrollbar_track_v1.png
- assets/source/ui/events/shop/secondary/shop_scrollbar_thumb_v1.png

Purpose:
- Scrollbar art for large player deck lists in copy-card, remove-card, and choose-card flows.
- Track and thumb must be separate pieces.
- Scroll position and thumb height are controlled by the game UI layer.

Proportion:
- Track: very narrow vertical rail, about 0.12:1 width-to-height; intended canvas 128x1024.
- Thumb: compact vertical handle, about 0.25:1 width-to-height; intended canvas 128x512.
- The sheet should show the track and thumb separately with generous spacing.

Structure:
- Track: thin dark teal/navy gothic rail with antique-gold edge lines and subtle stained-glass depth.
- Thumb: small movable gold/teal handle with restrained jewel accent and clear grip shape.

Critical rules:
- No arrows unless extremely subtle and blank.
- Prefer flat or jewel-capped ends over obvious arrowheads, so the scrollbar does not imply a clickable arrow button unless code implements one.
- No text.
- No Chinese characters.
- No numbers.
- No icons.
- No fixed scroll position.
- No baked list background.
- Track and thumb must be visually compatible but not connected.
- Keep ornamentation thin so the scrollbar does not steal attention from cards.

Style:
- Questers-specific 1990s Japanese cel-animation fantasy UI.
- Dark cathedral stained glass.
- Deep navy and teal enamel.
- Delicate antique-gold filigree trim.
- Thin gothic borders.
- Hard ink outlines.
- Aged hand-painted texture.
- Restrained blue jewel nodes.
- Candlelit gold highlights.
- Match existing Questers card and relic art.
- Avoid modern browser scrollbar styling and chunky mobile-game UI.

Background:
- One uniform #ff00ff background only.
- No shadow.
- No gradient.
- No texture.
- No floor plane.
- No lighting variation.
- Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the asset artwork.
